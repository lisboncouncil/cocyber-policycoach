## Information Security Policy for a Small Hospital (Sardinia, Italy)

**Policy Number:** ISP-2024-001
**Date Issued:** October 26, 2023
**Revision Date:** N/A
**Applies To:** All employees, contractors, and anyone accessing hospital information systems.

**1. Purpose**

This Information Security Policy establishes a baseline level of security for the protection of patient data, hospital systems, and overall operational integrity. It outlines the responsibilities of all personnel in maintaining a secure environment, compliant with relevant Italian regulations and industry best practices.  This policy is particularly relevant given the small size of the hospital and the limited resources dedicated to IT security.

**2. Scope**

This policy applies to all information assets owned, managed, or accessed by the hospital, including but not limited to:

*   Electronic Health Records (EHR)
*   Medical Devices connected to the network
*   Computer Systems and Servers
*   Network Infrastructure
*   Mobile Devices used for hospital business
*   Data stored in any format (electronic, paper, etc.)

**3. Policy Statements**

*   **Confidentiality:** Patient data and other sensitive information must be protected from unauthorized access, use, disclosure, disruption, modification, or destruction.
*   **Integrity:** Information must be accurate, complete, and reliable. Systems and processes must be in place to prevent unauthorized modification or deletion of data.
*   **Availability:**  Systems and data must be accessible to authorized personnel when needed to provide patient care and maintain hospital operations.
*   **Access Control:** Access to information and systems will be granted based on the principle of least privilege – individuals will only have access to the information and systems necessary to perform their job duties.
*   **Incident Response:**  A documented incident response plan will be maintained to address security breaches and other security incidents.
*   **Compliance:** The hospital will comply with all relevant Italian data protection laws and regulations, including but not limited to those related to healthcare data.

**4. Responsibilities**

*   **Hospital Administration:** Responsible for providing adequate resources and support for information security initiatives.
*   **IT Team (2 person team):** Responsible for implementing and maintaining security controls, monitoring systems for security threats, and responding to security incidents.
*   **All Personnel:**  Responsible for adhering to this policy and reporting any suspected security breaches or vulnerabilities.

**5. Key Security Controls**

*   **Antivirus/Malware Protection:** All systems will be protected with up-to-date antivirus and malware protection software.
*   **Firewall:** A firewall will be implemented to protect the hospital network from unauthorized access.
*   **Password Management:** Strong passwords will be required for all systems and accounts.
*   **Data Backup and Recovery:** Regular data backups will be performed to ensure data can be recovered in the event of a disaster or system failure.
*   **Security Awareness Training:**  All personnel will receive security awareness training to educate them about security threats and best practices.

**6. Policy Review**

This policy will be reviewed and updated at least annually, or as needed to address changes in the threat landscape or regulatory requirements.
