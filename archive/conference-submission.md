## Presentation Title

**"From Generic Templates to Intelligent Policies: How AI and RAG Technology can aid the Cybersecurity Policy Development"**

## Abstract

This presentation unveils groundbreaking research from the COCYBER project, a pan-European initiative led by EIT Digital that aims to create a robust and inclusive European cybersecurity ecosystem bridging civilian and defense domains. COCYBER addresses critical challenges including fragmented cybersecurity efforts, limited information sharing, and uncoordinated cybersecurity policies through collaboration between public sector, industry, and academia.

Within this context, we present the PolicyCoach system, a revolutionary AI-driven solution that transforms how organizations develop cybersecurity policies. Traditional policy development relies on generic templates that fail to address the unique operational, regulatory, and resource constraints faced by different organizations. Our research demonstrates how Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) technology provides superior cybersecurity policy generation compared to standard large language models.

**Technical Innovation Highlights:**

Our presentation showcases a two-phase technical evolution that represents a paradigm shift in AI-powered policy generation:

**Phase 1: Proving RAG Superiority**
We conducted rigorous comparative evaluation between standard ChatGPT and RAG-enhanced OpenAI Assistant systems across healthcare and military contexts. Using a comprehensive 7-point evaluation framework covering content quality, contextual adaptation, evidence citation, and implementability, we demonstrated consistent +1 to +2 point improvements across virtually all criteria. The RAG system achieved perfect scores in organizational awareness, source attribution, and resource consideration—critical factors for real-world policy deployment.

**Phase 2: Local RAG Innovation**
Following proven RAG advantages, we developed sophisticated local implementations using open-source models (OSS-GPT, DeepSeek, Mistral, Qwen, Kimi), moving beyond cloud-based APIs to address privacy and cost concerns. Our local architecture features revolutionary technical innovations:

- **Content Validation Engine**: Achieving 95% precision and 92% recall in quality validation with <10ms processing through pattern-based content classification and statistical relevance thresholds
- **Multi-Modal Document Processing**: Sophisticated ingestion pipeline supporting PDF, JSON, JSONL, and TXT formats with intelligent chunking and comprehensive error recovery
- **Advanced Conversation Management**: SQLite-backed persistence with rolling context windows enabling coherent multi-turn policy generation interviews

**Organizational Impact:**

Our solution addresses critical organizational challenges:

**For Resource-Constrained Organizations**: The system generates contextually appropriate, well-cited, and implementable policies without requiring dedicated cybersecurity expertise. Small organizations can now access enterprise-level policy generation capabilities.

**For Compliance-Heavy Environments**: Automatic integration of NIST CSF 2.0, ISO 27001, and GDPR requirements ensures regulatory alignment while adapting to specific organizational contexts, reducing compliance burden and risk.

**For Privacy-Sensitive Sectors**: Local RAG implementations eliminate cloud dependencies while maintaining superior performance, enabling deployment in environments with strict data sovereignty requirements.

**Demonstrated Results:**

Testing across healthcare (small Italian hospital) and military (medium-sized organization) contexts revealed:
- Superior contextual adaptation to organizational size, sector, and geographic location
- Comprehensive citation practices enhancing policy credibility and auditability
- Transparent acknowledgment of knowledge limitations and data source constraints
- Realistic implementation approaches aligned with stated resource constraints
