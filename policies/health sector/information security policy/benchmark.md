# Healthcare Information Security Policy
by GPT
## 1. Purpose
This policy defines the information security requirements for protecting the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of patient and organizational data at [Hospital Name], in compliance with Italian and EU regulations including GDPR and the NIS2 Directive.
## 2. Scope
This policy applies to all employees, contractors, and third-party service providers with access to the hospital's information systems, data, and medical devices.
## 3. Information Security Governance
* The hospital shall maintain an Information Security Officer (ISO) responsible for implementing and monitoring security measures.
* The ISO reports to hospital management and coordinates with IT and clinical departments.
## 4. Data Protection
* All patient data must be treated as sensitive personal data under GDPR.
* Data encryption must be enforced both at rest and in transit.
* Access to electronic health records (EHRs) is role-based and audited.
* Data retention and deletion policies must follow national healthcare guidelines.
## 5. Network and System Security
* Firewalls, intrusion detection systems (IDS), and antivirus software must be deployed.
* All systems must be regularly patched and updated.
* Remote access is only allowed via VPN with multi-factor authentication (MFA).

## 6. Medical Devices Security
* All connected medical devices must be inventoried and assessed for vulnerabilities.
* Security patches from vendors must be applied promptly.
* Only authorized devices are allowed on hospital networks.

## 7. Incident Response
* An incident response plan must be established and tested annually.
* All security incidents must be reported immediately to the ISO.
* The hospital must notify relevant authorities and affected individuals in accordance with GDPR breach notification requirements.

## 8. Training and Awareness
* All staff must receive annual training on cybersecurity best practices and GDPR compliance.
* Phishing simulation exercises must be conducted at least biannually.

## 9. Third-Party Management
* Contracts with IT service providers must include specific data protection and cybersecurity clauses.
* Third parties must be vetted for compliance with ISO/IEC 27001 and GDPR.

## 10. Policy Review
* This policy shall be reviewed annually or upon significant changes in regulatory requirements or hospital operations.
