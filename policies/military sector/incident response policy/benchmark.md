# Incident Response Policy - benchmark
Military Organization

# 1. Purpose
This policy establishes the procedures for identifying, managing, and responding to cybersecurity incidents affecting the military organization based in Italy. It ensures alignment with NATO directives and ISO/IEC 27001 to protect classified documents and communications.
# 2. Scope
This policy applies to all personnel, contractors, and systems within the organization, particularly those handling classified data and communications.
# 3. Definitions
* **Cybersecurity Incident**: Any event that threatens the confidentiality, integrity, or availability of information assets.
* **Incident Response Team (IRT)**: Group of designated personnel responsible for managing incident response activities.

⠀4. Roles and Responsibilities
* **Commander**: Approves major response actions, allocates resources.
* **Designated IRT Leader (temporary until dedicated IT unit is established)**: Coordinates incident response activities.
* **All Personnel**: Required to report suspected incidents immediately.

⠀5. Incident Response Lifecycle
**1** **Preparation**
	* Identify temporary IRT from available personnel with relevant IT/cyber knowledge.
	* Conduct awareness training quarterly.
**2** **Identification**
	* Use manual monitoring and basic tools to detect incidents.
	* Report incidents within 1 hour of discovery.
**3** **Containment**
	* Isolate affected systems immediately.
	* Maintain operations of unaffected systems.
**4** **Eradication**
	* Remove malicious content and unauthorized access.
	* Apply patches and update configurations.
**5** **Recovery**
	* Restore systems from backups.
	* Monitor systems for signs of reinfection.
**6** **Lessons Learned**
	* Document incidents and response.
	* Conduct post-mortem reviews within 72 hours.

⠀6. Incident Classification
* **Low**: No data loss or operational impact.
* **Medium**: Limited data exposure or partial system disruption.
* **High**: Exposure of classified data or full system outage.

⠀7. Communication Protocols
* Maintain a secure communication channel for incident reporting.
* Notify NATO liaison and Italian national cybersecurity authorities for High-level incidents.

⠀8. Documentation and Reporting
* Maintain an Incident Log including:
  * Date/time of detection
  * Affected systems
  * Actions taken
  * Outcome and follow-up steps

⠀9. Review and Testing
* Test the incident response process bi-annually.
* Review and update this policy annually or after significant incidents.

⠀10. Compliance and Enforcement
Non-compliance with this policy may result in disciplinary action per military regulations.

*Sources:*
